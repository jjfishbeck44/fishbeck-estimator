# Referral Partners

Track relationships that send (or could send) business: realtors, property managers,
lenders, wholesalers, suppliers, insurance agents.

## Approach
- Make it easy to refer (clear ask, simple handoff)
- Reciprocate referrals
- Stay top-of-mind (content, check-ins)
